# Unity save fields

Layout, key and containers are in `FORMAT_ACU.md`. The reader is `Games/Acu` (read-only); typed models are in `AcuModels.cs` (`AcuGameData`), `AcuMissionModels.cs`, `AcuPlayerProgressionModels.cs`, `AcuOnlineModels.cs` and `AcuOptionsModels.cs`; the header is `AcuHeader`. Names follow `Names/names.txt`; every name in it for Unity was read from ACViewer screenshots and matched to the stored CRC32 key (confirmed). Keys ACViewer shows as `Property_0x<HASH>` count as named.

## Game save, main container
- Top-level items: ContextID, NarrativeMissionHistory, SideMissionHistory, CurrentRoot, CurrentMission, TrackedMission, World, PlayerPortal, TransitionProfile, AnimusDatabaseData, MapFogData, MessagingDatabaseData, TrainingData, CaseFileData, PresentEmailsData, AccomplishmentManagerData, PlayerProgSaveData, SnapshotPlayerProgSaveData, SnapshotPlayerProgressionDelta, WorldUpgradeSaveData, SnapshotWorldUpgradeSaveData, VillaManagerSaveData, OnlineGameSaveData, ContactOnlineServiceSaveData, TimedCollectibleSaveData, OnlineGameEntitySaveData, PermanentIconSaveData, CrowdEventsSaveData, ClanManagerData, PushMatchmakingData. [confirmed]
- `AcuGameData.From` reads each of these into a typed model. Fields whose layout is not known (`CustomContacts`, `PlayerProgressionCharacterCustomization`, the PlayerCharacterItemsDelta entries) stay in the node tree.

## Mission history
- NarrativeMissionHistory and SideMissionHistory hold `Identifier` (enum MissionHistoryTrackIdentifiers), `GroupSaveData` (Group, `MissionSaveData`) and `LastConcurrentStepPortal`. Each mission has Mission, HighestSynchronization, TimePlayed, PersistentFlags, BriefingDisplayedOnce, BriefingAccepted, MissionStepSaveData, MissionConstraintSaveData (ConstraintID, PlayerSelected, IsRevealed, CurrentRunState, BestRunSucceeded, ConditionData with ConditionID and MetaValue) and ResetMissionConstraintSaveData. [confirmed names]
- HighestSynchronization holds 0, 1, 2 or 52 in the saves read; its meaning is not confirmed.

## Player progression
- `PlayerProgSaveData` and `SnapshotPlayerProgSaveData` have the same layout: UncollectedInventoryItemsOnMap, ProgressionCharacterData (3), PlayerProgressionCharacterDataSnapshots (2), QuestItemsCollectedAmounts (24), PlayedCollectiblesGlyph, StackOfLootMoney, DataInitialized, WorldNotorieties (WorldNotorietyList: NotorietyWorld, NotorietyValue, NotorietyLevel), MainWorldsHorse / OtherWorldsHorse / ReplayHorse (Mounted), GhostSave (FirstGhostSeen), SavedInventoryItems (IsLock, InventoryItems). [confirmed names]
- A character (PlayerProgressionCharacterData) holds ProgressionCharacterSelector, InventorySpecification (InventoryContainerList of Category, CurrentContainerLevel, CurrentItemCount, ItemList, OverflowQuantity, OverflowItemList), three lists shown as `Property_0x7063A919`, `Property_0xBFA174B0` and `Property_0x83F9AC79` (InventoryItemListEntries of Count and ItemDescriptor with Type, Item, RechargeableCategory), BackupInventoryItems (same item lists as the character), GainedInventoryItems, LockedInventoryItems, ArmorStates, selected weapon, equipment and ranged, QuickSlotItems and QuickSlotWeapons (ItemEnumValue, ItemEnumType), NewInventoryItems, MainAvatarLoadout (WaistSlot, ChestSlot, ForearmsSlot, HeadSlot, LegsSlot, MeleeWeaponSlot, RangedWeaponSlot, DLCItemSlot, CostumeSlot, ColorSlot, CurrentCoopSkill, CurrentSelectedBooster), TagAIAction, BrokenHealth, Life, MaxLife, AvatarSkills, AvatarSkillsUnlock, SpendedAvatarSkillPoint, LifetimeCreedPoints, CurrentCreedPointsIncrease, CurrentChallenge, DiscoveredMissions. [confirmed names]
- ID values (items, missions, categories) are the 64-bit ids as stored; the meaning tables are not built.

## Other objects
- `WorldUpgradeSaveData` (and Snapshot): GainedWorldUpgradeSettings, BuildingsSaveData (Buildings of Building and IsNewlyGained, BuildingsRules), CurrentGoldChestAmount, CurrentIncomeDelay, PendingIncomeValueForAfs. [confirmed names]
- `VillaManagerSaveData` > TrophyManagerSaveData: MissionConditionData (23), AccomplishmentConditionData (8), GearConditionData (GearLevels, 7 values), AchievementConditionData (UnlockedAchievementCount), LegacyConditionData (8), EliseLetterConditionData (5); the condition lists hold Unlocked and New. [confirmed names]
- `OnlineGameSaveData` > PlayerStatisticsSaveData: LocalStatistics and UnsentStatistics, each entry StatId and StatValue. [confirmed names]
- `OnlineGameEntitySaveData`: WorldMapOnlineEntity (Maps of Id and Districts with Id, ContractMissionsCompleted, TreasureHuntMissionsCompleted, MurderMysteryMissionsCompleted, ChestsCollected, CockadeCollected, ArtifactCollected, `Property_0x521DD66A` (S32), NodeState, SubDistrictsUnfogged), PlayerProgressionOnlineEntity (Hdbs, inProgressMissionId, CompanionRewardsClaimed, Missions of Id, IsCompleted, Constraints with Id, Progress, Verified, and Rewards, Gears, GlitchCollectedLocal, GlitchCollectedGlobal), BookmarkOnlineEntity, PlayerProfileOnlineEntity (Clan with Id and Tag, Gears, BoosterPurchaseHistory, MemoryCategories, FavoriteActivity, PlayerLevel, PlayerTitle, ClubWarDivision, FavoriteCoopSkill, PlayerPlayStyle). [confirmed names]
- Gears: Attack, Defense, Stealth, Support, Rating, PlayStyle and Items (Id, Status, Quantity, IsUpgraded). MemoryCategories entries: Title (OasisLineID), ProgressPercentage, LowSyncMedalPercentage, MediumSyncMedalPercentage, HighSyncMedalPercentage, QuestLogCategoryType. [confirmed names]
- `ClanManagerData` (ClanInvites, ClanNotifications, ClanTag, LastNotificationDate, LastApplicationDate, `Property_0x98CF025E`), `CaseFileData`, `TrainingData`, `TimedCollectibleSaveData` (CollectibleID, CollectedTimestamp), `ContactOnlineServiceSaveData`, `PermanentIconSaveData`, `CrowdEventsSaveData`, `PushMatchmakingData`. [confirmed names]

## Options save (slot 3)
- Profile container: PlayerOptionsSaveData (DisplaySubtitles, languages, volumes, Vibration, camera sensitivity and inversion, CameraInserts, Brightness, Blood, TimeOfDay, JoinInProgressGhosts, RiftGhosts, Companion and the HUD flags), AccomplishmentManagerData, OnlineProfileSaveData (EventsSaveData, ExclusiveContents, UplayRewards, UplayActions), RiftGhostData (Year, Week, MaxSeeds, BlackList, ScoreHistory), AssassinsRankTableData (31 AssassinsRankSaveData entries of RankIndex, RankThreshold, RankRewardID). Last container: SinglePlayerOptionsSaveData (FlyingMachineControls and the two cannon inversion flags). [confirmed names]
