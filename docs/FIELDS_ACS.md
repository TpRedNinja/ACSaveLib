# Syndicate save fields

Layout, key and containers are in `FORMAT_ACS.md`. The reader is `Games/Acs` (read-only); typed models are in `AcsModels.cs` (`AcsGameData`), `AcsMissionModels.cs`, `AcsPlayerProgressionModels.cs` and `AcsOptionsModels.cs`; the header is `AcsHeader`. Names follow `Names/names.txt`. A key whose name is shared with Unity was read in ACViewer on a Unity save and matches the same CRC32 key here (confirmed). 20 more are strings in `ACS.exe` whose CRC32 equals a key (likely). 27 names were read in ACViewer on a Syndicate save and match the CRC32 key (confirmed).

## Game save, main container
- Top-level items: ContextID, NarrativeMissionHistory, SideMissionHistory, CurrentRoot, CurrentMission, World, PlayerPortal, TransitionProfile, AnimusDatabaseData, MessagingDatabaseData, AccomplishmentManagerData, PlayerProgSaveData, SnapshotPlayerProgSaveData, SnapshotPlayerProgressionDelta, WorldUpgradeSaveData, OnlineGameSaveData, PermanentIconSaveData, CrowdEventsSaveData, DistrictManagerSaveData, ProgressionLogSaveGameData, TutorialData, CraftingData, BlackBoxData and LondonUpgradeData (each holds one unnamed packed list; 55, 51, 0 and 39 entries in the 100% save). [confirmed names for all but the last five]
- Not present compared with Unity: MapFogData, TrainingData, CaseFileData, PresentEmailsData, VillaManagerSaveData, TimedCollectibleSaveData, ContactOnlineServiceSaveData, OnlineGameEntitySaveData, ClanManagerData, PushMatchmakingData. Present only in Syndicate: DistrictManagerSaveData, ProgressionLogSaveGameData. [confirmed on the three game saves]

## Missions
- NarrativeMissionHistory and SideMissionHistory have the Unity layout (`Identifier`, `GroupSaveData`, mission steps and constraints) plus `MissionOutputSaveData` entries (`Output` and an unnamed key). The identifiers are the same as in Unity. [confirmed]
- HighestSynchronization holds small values (0, 1, 2, ...); its meaning is not confirmed. The 100% save has 30 narrative groups with 274 missions and 7 side groups with 73 missions. [confirmed against the file]

## Player progression
- `PlayerProgSaveData` and the Snapshot twin: UncollectedInventoryItemsOnMap, CurrentProgressionCharacter, LastLondonProgressionCharacter, AcquiredPerks, AcknowledgedPerks, ProgressionCharacterData (3 in the 100% save, 5 in the DLC save), DataInitialized, SavedInventoryItems (IsLock, InventoryItems). Each character: ProgressionCharacterSelector (ProgressionCharacter), InventorySpecification (InventoryContainerList), GainedInventoryItems, LockedInventoryItems, SelectedWeapon, SelectedEquipment, SelectedRanged, NewInventoryItems, TagAIAction, BrokenHealth (S32), Life (S32), MaxLife (S32), CharacterCustomizationSpawnTag (Tags of Tag), UnlockedSkills, AcknowledgedSkills, AcquiredSkills, AcquiredSkillPoints, LifetimeCreedPoints (U64), CurrentCreedPointsIncrease (U64), DiscoveredMissions, XP, Tier, CanGainXP, CanEarnMoney. [confirmed names, except `Tags`, `Tier`: likely]
- `SnapshotPlayerProgressionDelta`: PlayerCharacterItemsDelta (one per character: ProgressionCharacter, ItemsDelta, GainedInventoryItemsDelta, PlayerCharacterConsumableQuantity entries of Category and Quantity, XP, AcquiredSkillPoints, AcquiredSkills, UnlockedSkills, AcknowledgedSkills), UncollectedInventoryItemsOnMapDelta, AcquiredPerks, AcknowledgedPerks. [confirmed names]
- The Unity fields AvatarSkills, AvatarSkillsUnlock, SpendedAvatarSkillPoint, MainAvatarLoadout, BackupInventoryItems, QuestItemsCollectedAmounts, WorldNotorieties and the horses are not present. [confirmed]
- ID values are the 64-bit ids as stored; the meaning tables are not built.

## Other objects

- `OnlineGameSaveData`: PlayerProfileSaveData and HeroStatsSaveData (Statistics of StatId and StatValue; 345 and 18 entries in the 100% save), SocialCollectablesSaveData, SecondaryStoreSaveData, LeaderboardsSaveData, CommunityHeroStatsSaveData, OnlineCoreGameSaveData. [names confirmed for Statistics, likely for the others]

[confirmed names]

## Options save (slot 3)
- Profile container: PlayerOptionsSaveData (as Unity plus TextLanguage, GPSVisibility and HUDMissionTrackerHidden), OnlineProfileSaveData (UplayRewards, UplayActions, UplayWelcomeBackDone, ULCUnlocksShown, TimeSaverXPBoostElapsedTime, TimeSaverXPBoostDuration, TimeSaverXPBoostIncreasePercent, ULCXPBoostElapsedTime, ULCXPBoostDuration, ULCXPBoostIncreasePercent, SecondaryStoreProfileSaveData with LastHelixBalance and LastExpiredTransactionTimestamp, OnlineCoreProfileSaveData with VirtualCurrencyProfileUpdated and EventSaveData). Last container: SinglePlayerOptionsSaveData. [names confirmed for the Unity ones, likely for the others]

## Open
- 29 distinct keys are unnamed over the six saves (`acs <file> unknown`: 4 in an options save, 24 and 25 in the game saves); they need ACViewer on a Syndicate save to be named. By object: SecondaryStoreSaveData 7, SinglePlayerOptionsSaveData 4, NarrativeMissionHistory 4 and SideMissionHistory 4 (same four keys), ProgressionLogSaveGameData 3, CartRaceSaveData 3, FightClubSaveData 2, TutorialData 2, MissionOutputSaveData 1, MissionSaveData 1, SocialCollectablesSaveData 1, and the single list in each of BlackBoxData, CraftingData and LondonUpgradeData (keys shared between objects are counted once per object).
- HighestSynchronization values, ID meaning tables, TimePlayed unit, layouts of fields empty in the samples.
